# MyStocky — Legal Documents

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for MyStocky, an offline-first iOS app for home inventory and storage management.

---

## 📱 About MyStocky

**MyStocky** is a mobile application that helps users map, manage, and track home inventory with features like:

- 2D storage mapping and location tracking
- Barcode scanning and photo capture
- Low-stock and expiry date alerts
- Storage basket and shopping list export
- Optional family sharing and multi-device sync (Premium)

**Platform:** iOS 17+  
**Model:** Freemium (Free tier capped at 30 items, 10 categories; Premium offers unlimited storage and multi-device sync)
